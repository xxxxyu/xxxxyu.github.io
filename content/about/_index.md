I'm a 5th-year Ph.D. candidate at the [Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University](https://air.tsinghua.edu.cn), advised by [Prof. Yunxin Liu](https://yunxinliu.github.io) and working closely with [Prof. Ting Cao](https://tingcao952.github.io). I received my B.E. in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2022. I was at [EESAST](https://eesast.com) and [NICS-EFC Lab](https://nicsefc.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn) as an undergraduate.

I work on **self-evolving physical AI (PSI, physical self-improvement)**: how agents can be deployed and act efficiently in the physical world, and continually improve through real-world interaction and feedback. I hope to connect algorithmic breakthroughs with the underlying infrastructure to accelerate this loop. Recent projects I have led or contributed to include:

- Training and inference infra ([OxyGen](https://air-embodied-brain.github.io/OxyGen), [Cosmos Lite](https://github.com/xxxxyu/cosmos-lite), [Embodied.cpp](https://github.com/SEU-PAISys/Embodied.cpp), [Patchwork](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33582))
- Runtime failure detection and in-context learning ([ActProbe](https://air-embodied-brain.github.io/actprobe), [Zeva](https://air-embodied-brain.github.io/Zeva))
- Closed-loop learning and agent improvement ([SteerDistill](https://air-embodied-brain.github.io/SteerDistill), [Zetta ζ](https://air-embodied-brain.github.io/zetta))

[OxyGen](https://air-embodied-brain.github.io/OxyGen) has been accepted to NeurIPS 2026, and [Zetta ζ](https://air-embodied-brain.github.io/zetta) has received over 1,000 GitHub stars.

My earlier work focused on efficient inference systems for edge devices ([FlexNN](https://github.com/xxxxyu/FlexNN), [Vec-LUT](https://github.com/OpenBitSys/vlut.cpp)). [Vec-LUT](https://github.com/OpenBitSys/vlut.cpp) received **MobiSys 2026 Best Paper Award Runner-Up** recognition. I also led the *Efficiency* section of the [Personal LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05459) survey, which has received over 500 citations.

Feel free to connect, exchange ideas, or collaborate: [lixiangy22@mails.tsinghua.edu.cn](mailto:lixiangy22@mails.tsinghua.edu.cn).
