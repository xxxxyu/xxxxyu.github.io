I began my Ph.D. at the [Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University](https://air.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) in 2022, advised by [Prof. Yunxin Liu (刘云新)](https://yunxinliu.github.io) and working closely with [Prof. Ting Cao (曹婷)](https://tingcao952.github.io). Before that, I earned my B.E. in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University.

My doctoral research focuses on **systems optimization for on-device AI inference**, with the goal of running frontier models efficiently on resource-constrained devices. Representative work includes **[FlexNN](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3636534.3649391)** (MobiCom 2024), **[Vec-LUT](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3745756.3809200)** (MobiSys 2026, **Best Paper Award Runner-Up**), and **[OxyGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14371)** (under review).

> An overview of my doctoral work: [MobiSys 2026 Rising Stars Forum](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3812835.3814828)

I am currently working on embodied AI, exploring **efficient inference and deployment** of embodied foundation models (VLAs and WAMs), as well as **self-evolving physical intelligence**.
